Strengthening Brand Perception Across Consumer Segments — Commuter, 
Gig Worker & Premium
Done by: Advait Sachin Keskar
THE PROBLEM
Eurogrip is well known, but each rider group wants something different. 
How do we strengthen the brand for all three and still stay one brand?
OUR IDEA
One promise, “Grip you can trust”, proven differently for each rider. One 
logo and look; only the message and channels change.

2. Budget, rollout and targets
BUDGET: ₹60 CR A YEAR
₹51 cr across the three segments plus ₹9 cr shared brand 
work. Digital 40%, Retail 30%, On-ground 30%.
ROLLOUT: 12 MONTHS
Months 0–3 pilot in Chennai and Bengaluru; 4–9 scale to 10 
cities; 10–12 review and reallocate.
TARGETS (PROPOSED)
Tyre finder in 5,000 shops; 50,000 Rider Cards; 100 club 
trials; premium consideration up (survey).
Note: budget is about 1.8% of TVS Srichakra’s FY26 revenue (company-wide, so an assumption); pilot cities and targets are proposals.
