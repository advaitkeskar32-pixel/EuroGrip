# Project Overview

EuroGrip is an interactive strategic marketing and AI-assisted decision-support platform designed to strengthen a motorcycle tyre brand's positioning across three major rider segments.

The platform addresses an important marketing challenge: different motorcycle riders have different expectations from a tyre brand. A single marketing strategy may not effectively address all customer groups.

EuroGrip brings these audiences together under one unified brand identity while enabling segment-specific marketing strategies, budget planning, campaign simulations and analytical insights.

# Problem Statement

Motorcycle riders have different usage patterns, priorities and purchasing expectations.

EuroGrip focuses on three major customer segments:
1. Commuters

- Primary requirements: Reliability, affordability and product availability.

2. Gig Workers

- Primary requirements: Durability, high mileage and reduced downtime.

3. Premium Riders

- Primary requirements: Superior grip, handling and performance.




The objective is to develop a targeted marketing strategy that addresses these requirements while maintaining consistent brand positioning.

### 1. Interactive Campaign Workspace

A centralized workspace for planning and managing marketing strategies.

- Adjustable annual marketing budget
- Rider-segment prioritization
- Marketing channel allocation
- Campaign constraints
- Quick-start strategy options
- Campaign recommendations

### 2. Scenario Comparison

Explore and compare alternative campaign strategies.

- Compare different budget allocation approaches.
- Evaluate projected audience reach.
- Compare rider uptake and trial capacity.
- Examine execution risks.
- Identify suitable strategies based on selected constraints.

### 3. AI & Machine Learning Intelligence Lab

The AI Lab demonstrates multiple analytical approaches:
The AI Lab demonstrates the following analytical approaches:

1. K-Means Clustering

- Groups riders based on daily usage and performance-affinity characteristics.

2. Marketing Mix Optimization

- Uses Gradient Descent concepts to explore optimal marketing budget allocation.

3. Predictive Tyre-Health / Remaining Useful Life (RUL) Modelling

- Estimates tyre failure risk and remaining useful life based on usage and tyre-condition parameters.

4. Cosine Similarity Recommender

- Identifies similarities between rider or tyre profiles to support recommendations.

5. Monte Carlo Simulation

- Explores possible campaign outcomes under different assumptions and uncertainties.

6. Campaign Strategy Copilot

- Supports campaign planning and generates segment-specific strategic recommendations.


### 4. Campaign Performance Tracker

Monitor campaign progress through an interactive performance dashboard.

- KPI selection
- Target-versus-actual comparison
- Performance summary cards
- Monthly progress visualization
- Campaign target monitoring

### 5. Custom CSV Data Explorer

Explore rider or campaign datasets through browser-based CSV processing.

- Upload CSV files up to 5 MB
- Inspect dataset columns
- Review rider-segment distribution
- Generate numerical summaries

### 6. Campaign Report Generation

Generate reports based on campaign workspace selections.

- Campaign strategy summary
- Rider-segment prioritization
- Budget distribution
- Marketing recommendations
- Roadmap and projected KPIs
- CSV download and print/save as PDF

##  12-Month Strategic Roadmap

EuroGrip proposes a three-phase implementation strategy.

1. Phase One – PROVE (Months 0–3)
- Pilot marketing campaigns in Bengaluru and Chennai.
- Understand rider preferences and market requirements.
- Evaluate initial campaign performance.
2. Phase Two – SCALE (Months 4–9)
- Expand successful initiatives to ten cities.
- Strengthen dealer engagement.
- Increase rider participation and campaign reach.
3. Phase Three – REALLOCATE (Months 10–12)
- Evaluate overall campaign performance.
- Optimize marketing budget allocation.
- Identify opportunities for the next strategic cycle.

##  Project Architecture

EuroGrip follows a browser-based architecture.

1. **Presentation Layer:** HTML and CSS provide the user interface.
2. **Interaction Layer:** JavaScript manages user inputs, navigation and campaign workflows.
3. **Analytics Layer:** Client-side algorithms perform segmentation, optimization, similarity calculations and simulations.
4. **Data Exploration Layer:** CSV files are processed locally in the browser.
5. **Reporting Layer:** Campaign information is presented through downloadable CSV and browser-based PDF printing.

Data & Model Limitations

EuroGrip is a strategic decision-support prototype.

- The campaign targets, budgets and projected outcomes are proposed planning values.
- Certain performance indicators use illustrative or simulated data.
- AI/ML demonstrations use configured or synthetic inputs where applicable.
- Predictive outputs should not be interpreted as validated real-world tyre failure predictions.
- CSV exploration is performed locally in the browser and does not imply centralized customer-data storage.

The platform demonstrates how data-driven analytics can support marketing decisions; actual business outcomes would require real campaign, rider and operational data.
